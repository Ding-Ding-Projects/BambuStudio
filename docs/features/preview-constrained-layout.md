# Preview layout in constrained space

Preview notifications use the actual available width before calculating their text lines. When a readable card fits beside the legend, it preserves that margin; otherwise it remains on the canvas in front of the legend, without overtaking an open menu. A tall card can scroll. The stack reserves a measured area for two keyboard-operable overflow actions: next notifications and notification history. Deferred notices remain in the live collection with their callbacks, and their display and fade timers pause while deferred. The existing notification centre retains its established event history.

The slicing-progress card measures the full localized status and calculates the current height before positioning itself. Its tips retain their original internal layout inside a bounded scrolling viewport, including the controller and links. The preview legend can expand at narrow widths; its existing horizontal and vertical scrolling keep details reachable. A status chip that does not fit beside the plate strip appears as wrapped text in the legend header. Grouping help moves to another row and wraps when necessary, with keyboard activation retained. Statistics-thumbnail text wraps and scales within the tile, with the full original label also available on hover or keyboard focus.
