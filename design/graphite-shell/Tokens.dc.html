Motion: easing tokens

{{e.name}}{{e.curve}}

Emphasized curves are drawn as paths in Material Design 3; the cubic approximations here are the ones the native animator can also evaluate. The spring is an overshoot approximation of the Expressive spatial spring, used only for position, size and shape, never for colour or opacity.
